FangCam 隐私政策

FangCam 由 Eric Fang 提供。这份政策说明 FangCam 1.0 如何处理你在 iPhone 上的拍摄内容和设备数据。

数据收集

FangCam 不要求创建账户，不含广告或分析服务，也不会向开发者服务器传输或收集你的照片、视频、音频、镜头预设或使用数据。

拍摄内容与本地数据

拍摄的照片和视频保存在设备的照片图库、App 本地文件，或你选择的外接存储设备中。你导入的 LUT、镜头预设、拍摄设置和诊断日志保存在设备本地。FangCam 不会自动上传这些内容。若你启用了 iCloud 照片或自行分享文件，相关传输由你的设备设置或所选服务管理。

设备权限

FangCam 仅在功能需要时请求相机权限以预览和拍摄，请求麦克风权限以录制视频声音，并请求照片权限以保存或查看拍摄内容、从照片中选择预设二维码。你可以在 iPhone“设置”中管理这些权限。

联系与变更

如有隐私问题，请发送邮件至 afangteam@gmail.com。如未来版本的数据处理方式发生变化，我们会更新本页和 App Store 的隐私信息。

FangCam Privacy Policy

FangCam is provided by Eric Fang. This policy explains how FangCam 1.0 handles your captures and device data on iPhone.

Data collection

FangCam does not require an account, include advertising or analytics, or transmit or collect your photos, videos, audio, lens presets, or usage data on a developer server.

Captures and local data

Photos and videos are saved to your device's photo library, local app files, or external storage you choose. Imported LUTs, lens presets, camera settings, and diagnostic logs remain on your device. FangCam does not automatically upload them. If you enable iCloud Photos or share files yourself, that transfer is governed by your device settings or the service you choose.

Device permissions

FangCam requests camera access for preview and capture, microphone access for video audio, and photo library access to save or view captures and select a preset QR code from Photos. You can manage these permissions in iPhone Settings.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions, email afangteam@gmail.com. If a future release changes how data is handled, we will update this page and the App Store privacy information.
